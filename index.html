For teachers: how this works

Three levels of detail

The Detail switch keeps the same model running underneath. Big picture gives the main idea in everyday words. Closer look adds more structures and variables. Deep dive adds the numbers and the harder reasoning. Any student can choose any level, and names never change between levels.

Ways in

  • Guided: one step at a time.
  • Full lab: every variable and an investigation.
  • Review: quiz-style questions with worked solutions.

Virtual labs: your handouts, on a virtual bench

Students read the instruments, record their own data, answer the questions and export a report to upload to Canvas.

  • Cranial nerve assessment, and myotomes and dermatomes
  • Manual muscle testing practical, shoulder and lower body

Put a tool in Canvas

Open any tool and use Embed this tool in Canvas at the bottom. Choose where it starts, copy the code and paste it into a Canvas page.

Privacy

Everything runs in the browser. There are no logins, and no student data leaves the device. An anonymous page counter (no cookies, no names) records which tools are opened and whether the visit came through a school device.
